# Description
This database was created by Rewant Bhriguvanshi. They collected their data from Wikipedia catalogs and OpenSearch API. The dataset covers movies released between the years of 2024-2026 along with their titles, release dates, box office stats, etc. The data is only inclusive of movies released in the United States. Some conflicting records appeared when looking at ratings due to a column titled “rating out of 10”. The dataset author says they used an “audience rated forecast” which varies from ratings listed from IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes. The dataset also includes movies that have not yet been released, which can affect the scoring of the movie and makes it an automatic “No” for being available for streaming.

# Reporting Question
Does the popularity of a movie contribute to whether a movie is available on Netflix? This information is useful to the audience because it will tell them how to easily access the movie and if other audience members enjoyed the film. After looking at all of the movie stats, if the dataset audience wishes to watch the movie, they do not have to waste their own time searching through Netflix only for it to not be there.
Based on my additional research, the rating of the movie does not necessarily impact whether the movie is available on Netflix or not, as many high rated movies were not on the platform.
