# Changes I made to The Eras Tour visualization

For this visualization of Taylor Swift's album release and tour timeline, I tried to reduce clutter but using a simple lollipop chart. This way the viewer can see the time gaps between the album release date and the start of the tour without having multiple bars representing the two variables. I decided to move the years to the X axis because I think it shows a clearer progression of time and better represents how quick the turnaround for a tour was after an album release. I also chose to change the amount of information given to the viewer to allow them to draw their own conclusions. The title I chose highlights the significant change in the data but doesn't offer the analysis that the original did.

# Changes I made to the world's deadliest animals visualization 

I chose to represent this data in a pie chat because I think it better shows that each animal contributes to the total instead of being a stand alone statistic. My complete idea for this chat would be to have it start as a picture of the Earth and the viewer would be able to interact with it by sliding and revealing each animal. I think it would be engaging while also representing that these are estimates taken from around the world, not just one region. I also changed the title to describe the most significant percentage of deaths caused by an animal.
